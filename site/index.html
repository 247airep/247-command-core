Buyer questions

Know what this is — and what it is not.

Is this another field-service platform?

No. The first review works from information you already have. The goal is to reduce what management reviews, not replace your current operating software.

Do you need direct access to our systems?

Not for the initial review when an export is sufficient. CSV or Excel is ideal.

Do you guarantee margin improvement or savings?

No. We identify evidence-backed management-review signals. Your team validates context and decides what action, if any, to take. Outcomes are measured afterward.

Why is the managed service $2,500 per month?

It is not a generic reporting subscription. It is recurring management-attention monitoring: new data is reviewed, signals are prioritized, management decisions and actions are tracked, and outcomes are measured so the system can become more useful over time.

What if nothing meaningful stands out?

We say so. The purpose is to find priorities, not manufacture problems.

Can we stop after the paid Priority Review?

Yes. Managed monitoring is optional and is offered only when the first review proves useful.
